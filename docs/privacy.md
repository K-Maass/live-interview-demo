# Data boundaries

The replay uses fictional meeting text and simulated provider responses. Its requests are handled in process.

In the macOS app, microphone and system audio are processed on the Mac. If online guidance is enabled, the managed service sends selected transcript and workspace text to the provider. The fast and deep adapters request `store: false`. The Jev structured-decision route sends no "do not store" option. Provider retention depends on the applicable service terms and configuration.
